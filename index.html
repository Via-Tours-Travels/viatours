Terms & Conditions

Last Updated: February 2026

Welcome to Via Tours & Travels. By accessing our platform and placing trip inquiries or bookings, you agree to comply with our client booking terms.


1. Services & Itinerary Consultations

Via Tours & Travels acts as an authorized luxury travel concierge, organizing custom itineraries, accommodations, private ground transport, and sightseeing tours.


2. Booking Confirmations

A customized itinerary quotation is finalized upon receipt of initial deposit. Full vouchers and day-by-day travel documentation are issued prior to travel.


3. Pricing & Currency

Quotations are provided in INR, USD, EUR, or AED depending on your selection. Flight and seasonal surcharges are transparently shared prior to confirmation.


4. Cancellations & Alterations

We work closely with 5-star partners with flexible cancellation policies, detailed transparently on each final quotation.
