Terms & Conditions

Last Updated: February 2025

Welcome to Via Tours & Travels. By using our website and submitting enquiries, you agree to the following terms and conditions:


1. Services Provided

Via Tours & Travels provides travel consultation, itinerary planning, and booking assistance for domestic and international tours. We act as a facilitator between you and various travel service providers (hotels, airlines, transport companies). We do not process online payments directly on our website at this time. All financial transactions will be handled directly between the client and our agency via verified bank transfers or payment links.


2. Bookings & Enquiries

When you submit a "Plan My Trip" enquiry, our team will contact you to discuss your requirements, provide a quotation, and finalize the itinerary. A booking is only considered confirmed after an advance payment is received and acknowledged by Via Tours & Travels.


3. Pricing & Taxes

All package prices are listed in Indian Rupees (INR ₹). Prices are subject to change based on hotel availability, flight fares, and seasonal surcharges. Applicable taxes (including GST) and government levies will be included in your final quotation.


4. Travel Documents

It is the sole responsibility of the traveler to possess valid identification, passports, and required visas. While Via Tours & Travels provides documentation assistance, we are not liable for the rejection of visas or delays caused by government authorities.


5. Cancellations & Refunds

Cancellation policies vary depending on the specific hotels, airlines, and third-party vendors involved in your itinerary. The exact cancellation terms will be shared with you at the time of booking confirmation. Advance payments are generally non-refundable for hotel and flight bookings during peak seasons.


6. Limitation of Liability

Via Tours & Travels shall not be liable for any delays, injuries, losses, or damages resulting from third-party service providers, natural disasters, political unrest, or personal negligence during the trip. Travel insurance is highly recommended for all travelers.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2025

Via Tours & Travels respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.


1. Information We Collect

When you use our "Plan My Trip" enquiry form or interact with our chatbot, we collect the following details:

  • Full Name
  • Email Address & Phone Number
  • Destination preferences, travel dates, budget, and hotel preferences

We do not collect sensitive financial data (like credit card numbers) on this website, as payments are not processed here.


2. How We Use Your Information

The information you provide is used strictly to:

  • Contact you regarding your travel enquiry and provide customized quotations.
  • Create and manage your travel bookings.
  • Provide customer support during your trip.

3. Data Security

Your data is securely stored using Supabase (a secure database provider) with strict Row-Level Security (RLS) protocols. Only authorized administrators at Via Tours & Travels have access to your personal contact details and enquiry history. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.


4. Third-Party Service Providers

To fulfill your travel plans, we may need to share specific details (like names and contact info) with trusted third-party service providers such as hotels, airlines, and ground transport companies. These providers are strictly prohibited from using your data for any other purpose.


5. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, or ask for it to be corrected or deleted. To make such a request, please contact us directly at hello@viatours.com.
